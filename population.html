Model Information

1. Unconstrained Growth

The population increases continuously without considering limiting factors.

P(t) = P0 · e^(rt)

2. Unconstrained Decay

The population decreases continuously without considering limiting factors.

P(t) = P0 · e^(−rt)

3. Constrained Growth

The population increases while considering the limitations of the population model.

4. Constrained Decay

The population decreases while considering the limitations of the population model.
